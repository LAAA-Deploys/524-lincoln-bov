524 Lincoln Ave is a ten-unit apartment property in Northwest Pasadena consisting of an eight-unit 1925 building that was fully renovated in 2025 and two detached accessory dwelling units completed the same year. The original building totals 5,392 SF on a 10,945 SF PSR3-zoned lot.

Six units are occupied and four are vacant, including both new ADUs. Scheduled income in this analysis includes all four at their asking rents. Ownership acquired the property in December 2020, completed the renovation and ADU program in 2025, and operates it with third-party professional management.

The property fits private investors seeking turnkey Pasadena rental product: a fully renovated building with two new-construction ADUs, four units of immediate lease-up income, and room to move the occupied units to market on turnover.

The property is positioned for private investors and 1031 buyers who want completed renovation work, new-construction income, and a manageable ten-unit operation.

Investment Highlights

  • Ten units: eight fully renovated apartments plus two detached ADUs completed in 2025
  • Originally built 1925 and fully renovated in 2025 inside and out
  • Four one-bedroom, four two-bedroom, and two new-construction two-bedroom ADU floor plans
  • Four vacant units, including both new ADUs, ready for immediate lease-up at asking rents
  • Occupied rents average $2,270 per month with room to move to market on turnover
  • 10,945 SF (0.25 acre) PSR3-zoned lot with on-site parking
Location Overview
Northwest Pasadena, minutes from Old Pasadena and the Rose Bowl

The property sits on Lincoln Avenue in Northwest Pasadena, within roughly a mile of Old Pasadena, the Rose Bowl and the Foothill Freeway.

The surrounding blocks are a mix of small apartment buildings and single-family homes, and leased one-bedrooms in the immediate area run $2,100 to $2,300 per month.

Pasadena's employment base, school system and walkable core support durable occupancy for well-maintained smaller properties.

Rent roll and operating figures for the original eight units are transcribed from the July 31, 2026 rent roll and July 2026 cash flow statement prepared by Focus Property Services Inc. The two ADUs do not yet appear on the property management rent roll.

Interior and exterior photography of the renovated building is published by the property's own management company; additional exterior photography was taken by the listing team in August 2026.
